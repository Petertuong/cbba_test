Rules

  • Each task is worth its value, minus 2% per second of driving. Cars go 10 m/s.
  • Each car does at most 2 tasks, and each task goes to one car.
  • Cars bid for the tasks they value most. In each round they swap bids, and the higher bid wins a conflict (equal bids: lower car number).
  • 2–5 cars, 1–10 tasks, values 1–100.
